#english#
Tabgha - Lake Shore
Tabgha is located on the north-western side of the Sea of Galilee, about 3 kms south of Capernaum. The name Tabgha is a corruption of the Greek HEPTAPEGON, which means the "Seven Springs".
This picture was taken from the Sanctuary of the Primacy of Peter.
